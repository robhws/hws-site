Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026

Home / Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Healthy Water Solutions LLC ("we," "us") collects through toxinfreewithrob.com, healthyh2osolutions.com, our free water guide and our emails, and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Information you give us: your first name and email address when you request our free guide, and anything you share when you book or attend a consultation (such as your address, water source, water report or test results).
  • Your choices in our emails: which topic links you click (water testing, filtration or hydrogen water), so we can send emails on that topic.
  • Usage information: pages you visit, how you found our site, your general location (city or region), device and browser type, collected through cookies and analytics tools.

We do not collect payment information. Purchases are made on the manufacturer's or retailer's own website, under their privacy policies.

How we use it

  • To send you the free guide and the emails you signed up for
  • To answer your questions and run consultations you book
  • To recommend water testing, filtration or water products that fit your situation
  • To understand which content is useful and improve our site and emails

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

We share information only with the services that help us run the business, and only what they need:

Cookies and analytics

Our site uses cookies and similar tools to measure visits and improve our content. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site will still work.

Affiliate links

Some links on our site and in our emails are affiliate or distributor links, including Amazon, Multipure, Enagic and Tap Score. If you buy through them, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. When you click through, that company's privacy policy applies.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe anytime using the link at the bottom of any email.
  • Ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing rob@healthyh2osolutions.com. We'll respond within 30 days.

How we protect and keep your information

We use reputable service providers and limit access to your information. We keep it while you're subscribed or working with us, and delete it on request. No online system is completely secure.

Children

Our site and emails are meant for adults. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you think a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update it here and change the effective date above.
