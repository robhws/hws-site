Is a Kangen K8 Worth It? A Water Specialist's Wake-Up Call for Parents

A certified water specialist and dad of three on microplastics, PFAS, filtration and hydrogen-rich water, and whether the K8 is worth it for your family.

Home Is a Kangen K8 Worth It?

Your kids drink the water from your tap every day. It's in their glasses, their cereal and their pasta. So switch to bottled water and you're safe, right?

Not even close. One widely reported estimate found the average person may take in up to about five grams of plastic a week. That's roughly the weight of a credit card, and much of it comes from what we drink. Researchers have since found microplastics in human brain tissue, and even in the placenta, meaning they can reach babies before they're born.

I'm a mechanical engineer and a WQA Certified Water Specialist in good standing, and I've earned the Molecular Hydrogen Institute's professional certification. I've spent the last six years studying this water. I'm not writing this to scare you. I'm writing it to wake you up, because what your family drinks every day matters more than most parents realize.

Bottled water isn't the answer

Plastic bottles shed tiny plastic particles into the water inside them, especially when they sit in a hot car or a warm garage. And microplastics don't travel alone. Research suggests they can act like sponges for other pollutants, including PFAS, the "forever chemicals" that build up in the body and are linked to serious health concerns.

So the bottled water many families buy to stay safe can carry the very contaminants they're trying to avoid.

Your tap water needs real filtration

Tap water that meets legal limits isn't the same as water that's ideal for a growing child. Depending on where you live, it can carry chlorine and chloramine byproducts, lead from older pipes, and PFAS. Legal limits are set for whole populations, not for your kids' smaller bodies.

That's why a random filter from a big-box store isn't enough. You need filtration that's actually certified to reduce the specific contaminants in your home's water. Every home is different, which is why I always start by looking at what's in yours.

Then take clean water to the next level: molecular hydrogen

Once your water is properly filtered, you can do something most people have never heard of. You can enrich it with molecular hydrogen, which many researchers consider one of the most remarkable antioxidants ever studied.

This isn't a fad. Interest took off in 2007, when a landmark study in the journal Nature Medicine showed hydrogen acting as a selective antioxidant. It targets the hydroxyl radical, one of the most damaging free radicals in the body, while leaving the beneficial reactive oxygen species your body needs alone.

Since then, researchers have published well over a thousand studies on hydrogen. They've looked at its effects on oxidative stress and inflammation in areas such as brain injury, gut health, neurodegenerative conditions and arthritis. And across all that research, hydrogen has shown an excellent safety profile, with no significant side effects reported.

Much of this research is still early, and it isn't proof that hydrogen water cures anything. But nearly two decades of growing science is a powerful reason to give your family the benefit of it.

Is it safe for kids?

Yes. Kangen drinking water is filtered water that has been enriched through electrolysis, and families with children drink it every day. Enagic recommends easing in with a milder setting for the first couple of weeks. The strong acidic and strong alkaline waters are for cleaning and the kitchen, not for drinking. If your child has a medical condition, check with your pediatrician first.
